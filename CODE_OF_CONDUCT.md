# Code of conduct

This is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and
discussions is expected to:

- be kind and patient, and assume good intent;
- criticise work, never people, and say what would make it better;
- keep to the subject of the issue or pull request;
- respect that the maintainer decides what goes into the project and may say no;
- never harass, insult, threaten or demean anyone, and never share anyone's private
  information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not
keep to this. To report a problem privately, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile
or GitHub's own reporting tools.
