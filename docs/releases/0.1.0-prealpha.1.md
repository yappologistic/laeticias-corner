The first pre-alpha of **Laeticia's Corner**: a cosy isometric pixel-art world you decorate at
your own pace. You are given a small corner of land at the edge of an old world, and what you
build decides who comes to stay; beyond it lie roads, rivers, harbours and three busy ancient
cities. There is no score, no timer and no way to fail.

This is an early build: it runs and is playable, and it has rough edges. Feedback and bug
reports are very welcome.

## Downloads

Windows and Linux. There is no macOS build yet.

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `laeticias-corner-0.1.0-prealpha.1-windows-x64.zip` | Windows 10 (version 1903 or newer) and Windows 11, 64-bit |
| `laeticias-corner-0.1.0-prealpha.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | Linux on x86_64 with glibc 2.35 or newer, or RHEL/AlmaLinux 9: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and later |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | the checksums of both archives |

Nothing needs to be installed: each archive holds the game, its assets, the SDL library and a
`README.txt`.

## First run

**Windows.** Unpack the whole zip (do not run the game from inside it), open the folder and run
`laeticias-corner.exe`. The build is not signed, so Microsoft Defender SmartScreen may show
"Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Saves go to
`%APPDATA%\Laeticia\laeticias-corner\`.

**Linux.** Unpack and start it from its folder:

```sh
tar xzf laeticias-corner-0.1.0-prealpha.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz
./laeticias-corner-0.1.0-prealpha.1-linux-x86_64/laeticias-corner
```

It runs on X11 and Wayland, with PipeWire, PulseAudio or ALSA for sound. Saves go to
`~/.local/share/Laeticia/laeticias-corner/`.

To check a download: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt --ignore-missing` (Linux) or
`certutil -hashfile <file> SHA256` (Windows).

## What works

- The home corner: painting ground, planting and placing pieces, rotating, moving, undo and
  redo; wishes, the sketchbook and the land opening up in five stages; visitors who come for
  what you build; Laeticia's day; the four seasons, weather, day and night; growing and picking
  vegetables; postcards (`P`).
- The world around it: the camera roams the whole map; Rome, Athens and Pārsa with their people,
  markets, festivals and traffic; the map (`M`) and fast travel; landmark cards and the journal.
- Sound and music by place, hour, season and weather.
- Saving on its own, several worlds, settings, the pause menu and the first-minute tour.
- Graphics: the game asks for SDL's GPU renderer on Vulkan for the Low, Medium and High looks
  (light, bloom, water, clouds, seasons by climate) and falls back to OpenGL, then software, with
  the Classic look. On a computer without a Vulkan driver it runs in Classic and the settings say
  that the other looks cannot be drawn there.

## Known problems

Please read these before reporting a bug.

- **How these builds were tested.** Both archives are built, tested and started by GitHub
  Actions on machines without a screen or a GPU: each unpacked archive prints its version, plays
  300 frames and draws two screenshots (the home region and Rome's Forum) with the software
  renderer; the Linux archive also on AlmaLinux 9, Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12. Windowed play,
  the GPU looks and sound output have been tried on one Linux computer from the source, and
  **never in a window on Windows**. Expect platform problems, and please report them.
- **Performance is not proven.** The frame-time budgets were missed in the three city centres on
  the development machine under load. Start-up has a long first frame (about 100 ms), and there
  is some pop-in while panning zoomed out to a quarter.
- **Walkers and hills.** People stand correctly on sloped ground but cannot yet walk across
  slopes, so much of the countryside outside the cities is empty of walkers. Large plateaus are
  still flat, and deep stream cuts stay as cliffs.
- **Nights.** Many city districts are dark at night on the GPU looks.
- **Seasons on older renderers.** On the OpenGL and software renderers (the Classic look without
  Vulkan) the world outside the home region has one look all year.
- **Smaller things.** A one-pixel flicker along waterfall lips while panning; the wrong place
  name at one region border in Persia; snow on terraces at one river crossing; a pontoon bridge
  drawn as two loose rafts; a boat dropping over a waterfall.
- **Windows.** The game is a windowed program without a console: `--version` and its log show
  only when its output is redirected (`laeticias-corner.exe --version | more`).
- **Saves.** Saves are meant to stay compatible with later versions, but this is a pre-alpha:
  keep a copy of your save folder if a world matters to you.

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue with the **Bug report** form at
<https://github.com/yappologistic/laeticias-corner/issues>: the version (the archive's name, or
`--version`), your system and graphics card, what you did, what you expected and what happened,
a screenshot for anything visual, and the log if you can get it. Security problems go privately,
as `SECURITY.md` says.
