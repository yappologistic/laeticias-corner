# Sources

What each design file relies on, with one line on what each source was used for.

**How to read this list.**
- **Consulted** means the page was read directly or through its search-result summary during
  research.
- Some authoritative sites (Encyclopaedia Iranica, Britannica, the British Museum collection)
  refused automated access. For those, only search summaries were available. They are marked
  *(summary only)*, and their facts are flagged for checking in each city's "Uncertainties"
  section.
- **Ancient texts** are cited by book and chapter from standard editions. They are all available
  in translation at the Perseus Digital Library (https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/) or LacusCurtius
  (https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/home.html). A human should check the quoted passages
  before card texts are final.
